Changes to notice

A change that lasts, becomes more intense or affects daily life may be a reason to check in.

Possible changes

  • Becoming more withdrawn or losing interest in usual activities.
  • Ongoing changes in sleep, energy or mood.
  • Finding school, friendships or everyday tasks harder to manage.
  • Expressing feelings of hopelessness or being overwhelmed.
Keep perspective: These signs can have many causes and are not a diagnosis. Ask gently, listen, and consider professional advice if concerns persist or affect daily life.

Start with a conversation

Choose curiosity over assumptions. Find a calm moment and give your teenager time to respond.

Try saying

“I've noticed you seem a little different lately. How have things been feeling for you?”

Other helpful prompts:

  • “Would you like me to listen, or help think through it?”
  • “Thank you for telling me.”
  • “What would feel supportive right now?”
Try to avoid: dismissing feelings, comparing them with others, jumping straight to solutions or blaming them.

Small action for today

Set aside 10–15 minutes to spend together without distractions. Let your teenager choose the topic.
